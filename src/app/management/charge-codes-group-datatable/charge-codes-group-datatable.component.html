To Create or Edit a Charge Code Group, select at least one Charge Code, then move it to the Charge Code Group section by clicking the Add button.

Every Charge Code Group must have a designated Parent Charge Code, which is always required to form the Vendor Product. Required Charge Codes within a group indicate that those codes must be included for the system to identify and create the Vendor Product. Other Charge Codes in the group are optional and do not need to be present to identify and create the Vendor Product, though they may be included in some form of the Vendor Product. Ensure that the correct Charge Codes are marked as Parent or Required by checking or unchecking the relevant options.

Remove Charge Codes: You can remove existing Charge Codes from the Group by selecting at least 1 and selecting the Remove button.
