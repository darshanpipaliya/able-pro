Cost Allocation by Vendor Product

Cost Allocation by Vendor Product provides a summary of all charges associated with a Vendor Product, including the total charges and percentage allocated to cost center structures. If a Vendor Product isn't fully allocated to cost center structures, any unallocated costs must be assigned to an appropriate cost center structure.

To do this, utilize the Allocation Summary to identify Vendor Products that aren't fully allocated, then examine the allocation details in the lower grid. Selecting the edit button will allow you to exist any existing Cost Center Structures for this Vendor Product or create new Cost Center Structures to ensure all charges in the Vendor Product are assigned at 100%.

Once all updates are complete use the Rerun button at the top of the page to run the Cost Allocations and view the changes. The invoice will not leave Cost Allocation without all charges being assigned at 100%

 Cost Center Structure Detail By Vendor Product

Cost Center Structures represent predefined rules utilized for the selected Vendor Product in the Cost Allocation by Vendor Product grid on this page. These structures dictate how and where charges for the Vendor Product are allocated within the system.

Rule Conditions

Cost Allocation is the process of allocating and ensuring 100% of the Vendor Product Charges are assigned to a Cost Center Structure. The Cost Center Structure defines how Charges related to the Service and Service Type of Vendor Products are assigned to Cost Centers. The Structure assigns the amount of the charge as a percentage, which can be allocated at 100% of amount multiple Cost Centers with the total adding up to 100%. All charges within a Vendor Product must be allocated at 100% in order for the cost allocation to be complete.

Cost Center Structure uses Assignment Type to determine where the rules are applied in the sequence listed below. Multiple rules can be run on a single Service or Service Type; however, the system cannot process rules if the allocation is over 100%.

  • Company - Charges from Vendor Products on a Service Number are assigned the Companies cost allocation rules for the Service and/or Service Type. These rules are applied uniformly across all service and/or service types for the Company, resulting in a straightforward cost center structure for the company. This is a simplified cost center structure. Example: All Vendor Products with a Service that is equal to: Data will be applied to cost center 45632 and 89956.
  • People - Some charges from Vendor Products on a Service Number are allocated to People based on predetermined cost allocation rules for the Service and/or Service Type. These rules may complement other cost allocation directives that allocate certain costs to Locations or even to specific Vendor Products. Example: All Vendor Products with a Service that is equal to: Mobility will be applied to the Primary User assigned to that Vendor Product.
  • Location - Some charges from Vendor Products on a Service Number are allocated to a specific Location based on predetermined cost allocation rules for the Service and/or Service Type. These rules may complement other cost allocation directives that allocate certain costs to People or even to specific Vendor Products. Example: All Vendor Products with a Service that is equal to: Local and a Service Type of: Business Line will be applied to the Primary Location assigned to that Vendor Product.
  • Vendor Product - Charges on specific Service Numbers that have specific vendor products are allocated based on predetermined cost allocation rules for the Service or Service Type of the Vendor Product assigned to the Service Number. These rules may complement other cost allocation directives that allocate certain costs to People or Locations. Each Vendor Product possesses its own set of rules, which are applicable to all other Vendor Products sharing the same service and, potentially, service type within the cost allocation framework. Example: An ISDN PRI Mobility will be applied to the Primary User assigned to that Vendor Product.
