Cost Centers
Many Customers have long lists of Cost Centers many of which that do not apply to Fixed, Mobile or Cloud communications. Cost Centers that are used for communications, can be marked 'Active' in the Cost Center module and can be assigned to different items for cost accounting. 'Inactive' Cost Centers cannot be assigned for cost accounting.
Cost Center - Status field
These are Cost Centers related to the Customers communication Costs in the system.
       Active - Cost Centers the Company is activity using
       Inactive - Cost Centers the Company has stopped using or are not using yet
