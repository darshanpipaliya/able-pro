Cost Center Repository
Many customers have extensive lists of Cost Centers, many of which are not relevant to the spend in the application. These customers may choose to load all Cost Centers and then move only the ones they use for spending into the application. To do this, search the Cost Center Repository for the relevant Cost Centers and mark them as 'Used' to make them available for assigning costs. Cost Centers marked as 'Used' are automatically set to 'Active' in the Cost Center module and can be assigned to various items for cost accounting. Cost Centers that are unmarked or explicitly marked as 'Do Not Use' will be excluded from the Cost Center tab.
Repository - Status field
These are all Cost Centers the Customers imported from a Customers ERP, Finance or Accounting systems. They may or may not have to do with communications.
       New - Imported Cost Centers and not listed as used or Do Not Use
       Used - Cost Centers to add to the available Cost Center list
       Do Not Use - Cost Centers purposely excluded from the available Cost Center list
Cost Center - Status field
These are Cost Centers related to the Customers communication Costs in the system.
       Active - Cost Centers the Company is activity using
       Inactive - Cost Centers the Company has stopped using or are not using yet
