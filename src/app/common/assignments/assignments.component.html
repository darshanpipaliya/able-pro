The Primary Location is the site where the service is located or assigned. When cost allocation is enabled, the Location may also be responsible for the associated expenses. Each inventory item can have only one primary assignment, which can be either a person or a location.

The Primary User or Contact is the individual who is the primary user or owner of the service. This person is responsible for managing the service and, when cost allocation is enabled, may also bear responsibility for expenses associated with it. Each inventory item can have only one primary assignment, which can be either a person or a location.
