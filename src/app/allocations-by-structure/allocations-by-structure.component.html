Cost Center Structure for Invoice

Cost Center Structures represent predefined rules utilized for the selected Vendor Product in the Cost Allocation by Vendor Product grid on this page. These structures dictate how and where charges for the Vendor Product are allocated within the system.

Use this page to help understand that Cost Centers used on this invoice and anything assigned to them. Using this page as a guide will help you understand the impact of any changes you are considering to the overall invoice. For example, if you are looking at correcting the allocation on 1 Vendor Product on Cost Center 897758 from 65% to 100% you can see all the records the change will affect here.
