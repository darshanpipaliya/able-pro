Importance of Invoice Charge Values

When processing invoices, it’s essential to understand the Total Current Charges in detail, as it reflects the current cost of the services you purchase and provides insights into how your spending is changing over time. Equally critical is the Amount to Pay, which the system determines based on the following factors:

  • Past Due Balance: Any outstanding balance that may need to be paid (if approved to pay)
  • Adjustments: Applied to the account to account for prior discrepancies or charges
  • Current Account-Level Charges: Charges not associated with specific services but tied to the overall account
  • Current Service-Level Charges: Charges specific to individual services

The system then compares the vendor provided value Total Amount Due from the invoice with the Total Current Charges and selects the lesser of the two values. If this amount is less than 0, the Amount to Pay is set to 0.

This calculation ensures that payments consider all applied credits, carried-forward balances, and any past-due obligations. By doing so, the system prevents overpayment, avoids carrying credits forward unnecessarily, and ensures older balances are resolved efficiently.

  • Total Current Charges: The total of all account-level charges and adjustments, combined with all service-level charges listed on the invoice.
  • Amount to Pay: The system determines this value by selecting the lesser of Total Current Charges or Total Amount Due. If the resulting value is less than 0, the Amount to Pay will default to 0.
  • Previous Charges: The Total Current Charges from the previous invoice period.
  • Difference Fields: Calculated by comparing the Total Current Charges with the Previous Charges.
  • Past Due: The outstanding balance for the account, as specified by the Vendor on the invoice.
  • Pay Past Due: If the Past Due Balance is included in the invoice for payment, the Total Current Charges and Amount to Pay will be adjusted to incorporate the Past Due Balance. This amount will be distributed across all services on the invoice. Selecting "Yes" indicates that the Past Due Balance has been added to the Total Current Charges.
  • Last Payment: The most recent payment, as specified by the Vendor on the invoice.
  • % Allocation: If Cost Allocation is enabled, this represents the percentage of charges on the invoice assigned to cost centers. – indicates Cost Allocation is not enabled.
