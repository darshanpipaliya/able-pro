Replace Contract: (Document Name - Internal Contract Number)

The following active Contract and Addendums will be marked Inactive and replaced by the new contract you are adding. All assigned inventory will be associated with the new contract.

 All Documents

The active contract and addendums listed may have inventory associated with them. The replace contract action will do the following:

  1. Mark all the listed documents Inactive
  2. Creates a new Active Contract
  3. Copy the contract terms and other information from the existing contract record to the new contract record for editing in an effort to help save time.
  4. Associates the existing inventory with the new contract record
