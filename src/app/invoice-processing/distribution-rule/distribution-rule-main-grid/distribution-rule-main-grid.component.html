Charges for Distribution

In order to publish an invoice to the customer all charges must be balanced to the Amount to Pay. This means the detailed charges we found in the invoice matches the Amount to Pay. This step will help you identify any differences in the invoice and add any credits or debits with a charge code in order to balance the detail with the Amount to Pay.

An after the Amount to Pay means the Past Due Balance has been selected to pay. The difference in the invoice is based on itemized charges found in the invoice and not the Past Due Balance included.
