The system will mark an invoice as 'No Charges' when there are no current charges, and the balance forward is $0 or negative, but there is still inventory on the account with an Active or Pending Disconnection status.

To resolve this, all inventory on the account must be updated to 'Disconnected' status. Once this is done, the record can be transitioned to 'Disconnected Account' status and then moved to 'Do Not Process' status.

‘Account Disconnected’ status indicates one of two scenarios:

  • There are no current charges, the balance forward is $0 or negative, and there is no active inventory associated with the account

  • The payable account number has been marked as ‘Disconnected’

Add a note to the account, which will be added to both the invoice record and the account record. If necessary, this will also update the account status to ‘Disconnected’ and clear any open retrieval records.
