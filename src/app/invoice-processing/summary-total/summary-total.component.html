Amount to Pay

This is a system-generated field that represents the lesser of two values: Total Current Charges or Total Amount Due. If the lesser value is negative, it will be set to zero.

The Amount to Pay may also be adjusted if the "Pay the Past Due Balance" option is set to "Yes." In this case, the Amount to Pay will increase by the Past Due amount.

Please note: Total Current Charges are calculated as the sum of Adjustments and the Total Current Charges applied to this invoice.
