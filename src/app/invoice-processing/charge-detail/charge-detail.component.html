VBA for Charge Detail

 Vendor Billing Alias and Vendor by Charge Codes

The Vendor Billing Alias (VBA) is the verbatim name of the Vendor of record that is directly providing the service exactly as it is presented in the invoice. Whereas a Vendor name is considered the common name or "friendly" name for a provider, the Vendor Billing Alias reflects the Vendor name exactly as it is written in the invoice. Sometimes the Vendor Billing Alias will match the Vendor.

Examples:

AT&T
Vendor Billing Alias: AT&T Corporation
Vendor: AT&T

Lumen
Vendor Billing Alias: Lumen Technologies, Inc.
Vendor: Lumen
The use of Vendor Billing Aliases applies not only to the invoice, but also to individual charges on the invoice. These are often the same as the invoice Vendor Billing Alias but not always. In some cases, a third-party may be billing for services on another Vendor's invoice, which would cause the Charge Vendor Billing Alias to not match the Vendor Billing Alias of the invoice.

OAN
Charge Vendor Billing Alias: OAN
Invoice Vendor Billing Alias: SBC
Vendor: AT&T
Reason: OAN bills their 1+ service via AT&T's invoice from the old SBC Area
