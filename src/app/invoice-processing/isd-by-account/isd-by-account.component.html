Invoice Summary to Detail Charge Validation - By Account

To publish an invoice to the customer, the detail charges must match the Amount to Pay, meaning the detailed charges listed in the invoice must align exactly with the payment amount. Step 4 helps identify discrepancies by calculating the difference between the Amount to Pay and the detailed charges collected from the data files. This allows you to add any necessary credits or debits, along with a charge code, to reconcile the details with the Amount to Pay.

Use the following pages to identify missing charges by Account, Charge Location, and Billing ID. When possible, the system will highlight specific data points that are out of balance. However, if the Vendor does not provide totals by Billing ID, discrepancy information may not be available for certain sections. In such cases, a thorough review of all charges will be necessary. If totals are unavailable, the Difference field will display --.

Special Note: If the Amount to Pay includes the Past Due Balance, it indicates that the Past Due Balance has been selected for payment. In this scenario, discrepancies will be calculated based on the itemized charges in the invoice, excluding the Past Due Balance.
