Every Charge Code Group must have a designated Parent Charge Code, which is always required to form the Vendor Product. Required Charge Codes within a group indicate that those codes must be included for the system to identify and create the Vendor Product. Other Charge Codes in the group are optional and do not need to be present to identify and create the Vendor Product, though they may be included in some form of the Vendor Product.

If this group is incorrect for the listed Vendor Product, update it by selecting the Vendor Product Name and then selecting the group within the Vendor Product. After correcting the Charge Code Group on the listed Vendor Product by adding or removing Charge Codes or Deactivating the Group you assign your current invoice.
