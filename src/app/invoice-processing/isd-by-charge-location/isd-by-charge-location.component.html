Invoice Summary to Detail Charge Validation - By Charge Location

When an invoice is out of balance, research is necessary to identify and resolve the discrepancy. This page is designed to simplify the research process and save time where possible.

Types of Discrepancies

  1. Service Level Charges
    1. A discrepancy in the Service Charges section indicates that charges billed directly to the service or inventory are out of balance. The system will display the amount of imbalance.
  2. Account Level Charges
    1. A discrepancy in the Account Charges section means that charges not directly billed to the service but included in the current charges are out of balance. The system will show the imbalance amount.
  3. Account Level Adjustments
    1. A discrepancy in the Adjustments section signifies that charges applied to the previous balance are out of balance. The system will indicate the extent of the discrepancy.

Reviewing and Correcting Discrepancies

Clicking the link for any section with a discrepancy will display detailed information for that section. Differences will be highlighted to aid in reviewing and making corrections.

Vendor-Specific Limitations

In some cases, the Vendor may not provide totals by Billing ID, making it impossible to display discrepancy information for certain sections. When this occurs, a comprehensive review of all charges will be required. If totals from the Vendor are unavailable, the Difference field will display --.
