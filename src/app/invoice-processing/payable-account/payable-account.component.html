Invoice Summary to Detail Charge Validation - Payable Account

To publish an invoice to the customer, all charges must match the Amount to Pay. This means that the detailed charges listed in the invoice must correspond exactly to the Amount to Pay. This step helps identify any discrepancies in the invoice, allowing you to add any necessary credits or debits with a charge code to balance the details with the Amount to Pay.

When the Amount to Pay includes the Past Due Balance, it means that the Past Due Balance has been selected for payment. The discrepancies in the invoice will be based on the itemized charges found in the invoice, excluding the Past Due Balance.
