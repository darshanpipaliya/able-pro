Sandbox Status Description
No Vendor Vendor needs to be assigned to the Invoice in order to look for a matching Retrieval Record.
No Retrieval Record No Invoice Retrieval Record was found with a matching Account. Or the Retrieval Record is found but has a start date in the future.
Incomplete Retrieval Record We need to open the retrieval record and see what is going on. Likely there is no invoice attached to it.
No Charges This invoice has no current charges, and the Amount to Pay is $0.00 or negative. However, there is active inventory on the account that needs to be updated to Disconnected status in the system.
Disconnected Account This invoice has no current charges, with an Amount to Pay of $0.00, negative, or is for a disconnected account.
Pending The Invoice and Data Files or Invoice Upload template are available to process through the sandbox.
Processing The invoice is being que’d for the sandbox.
Working The Invoice and Data Files or Invoice Upload template are ‘Accepted for work’ and being worked actively.
Completed Sandbox work is complete and ready for Publishing.
Publishing The invoice is in the process of being sent to Production after the sandbox processes were completed.
Published The invoice has been sent to Production after the sandbox processes were completed and accepted.
Unpublished This status indicates that the invoice was previously published but required changes that necessitated its removal from production and return to the sandbox for updates. An Unpublished status signifies a priority invoice.
Priority Working This status indicates that an Unpublished invoice is actively being updated by someone to prepare it for republishing.
Do Not Process This is an invoice for an account that has been disconnected, and it is either the final processed invoice or no longer being processed.
Error This is an invoice that had a problem with the backend processing.
Data Issue An issue with Vendor or our data import has been identified for this invoice and we are working to resolve it.
Deleted Data file received but will not be processed, either because it was sent in error or the vendor has not yet stopped sending invoices.
