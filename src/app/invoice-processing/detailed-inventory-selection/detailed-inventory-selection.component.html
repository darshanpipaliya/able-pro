Add/Edit from Invoice

Assign a Vendor Product to selected Charge Codes in the current invoice. A Vendor Product's Charge Code Group may include multiple Billing IDs. Use this when all items that need to be assigned are not related to another invoice or account and all charge codes needed are within the invoice in context.

How to Use:

  1. Select all Charge Codes that:
    • Belong to the Vendor Product being added to inventory
    • Are associated with the Vendor Product
  2. Click Add/Edit from Invoice

Charge Codes Included:

  • Charge Codes included in a Vendor Product: Product, Feature, Usage, or Equipment (only one Product-type Charge Code per Vendor Product)
  • Related Charge Codes: Taxes, regulatory fees, carrier fees, discounts, refunds, etc. on another Billing ID when the system cannot establish the relationship automatically

Add/Edit from Inventory

Assign a Vendor Product to selected Charge Codes and link them to Billing IDs on Inventory from the same Vendor. This will allow you to add a vendor product from the same or another account to the invoice. Use this for scenarios like disconnected inventory or vendor invoices for separate services (e.g., site visits, construction). The resulting Vendor Product will include multiple Charge Codes.

How to Use:

  1. Select all Charge Codes that:
    • Need to be linked to another invoice to retrieve the Vendor Product
    • Are associated with the Vendor Product
  2. Click Add/Edit from Inventory

Charge Codes Included:

  • Charge Codes included in a Vendor Product: Product, Feature, Usage, or Equipment (only one Product-type Charge Code per Vendor Product)
  • Related Charge Codes: Taxes, regulatory fees, carrier fees, discounts, refunds, etc. on another Billing ID when the system cannot establish the relationship automatically
